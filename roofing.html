New roofs

New roof installation and re-roofing

A good roof starts below the sheets. We assess the structure, replace rotten timber and install a roof that drains properly and resists wind uplift.

  • Inspection of trusses, purlins and brandering
  • Replacement of damaged or rotten timber
  • Underlay / sarking and insulation options
  • Correct fixing patterns and sealing washers
  • Ridge capping, valleys, flashings and wall abutments
  • Gutters, downpipes, fascias and bargeboards
  • Safe removal and disposal of old roofing
Repairs

Roof repairs and leak detection

Most leaks start small: a cracked tile, a lifted flashing, a loose screw or a rusted sheet. Fixing them early saves ceilings, insulation and electrics.

  • Leak tracing at valleys, chimneys, skylights and abutments
  • Cracked, slipped or missing tile replacement
  • Ridge and hip re-bedding and repointing
  • Replacing corroded or loose sheets and fasteners
  • Re-sealing flashings, gutters and box gutters
  • Wind and storm damage repairs
Restoration

Roof restoration and roof painting

A tired roof does not always need replacing. Restoration can add years of life and transform the look of your property.

  • High-pressure cleaning to remove moss, lichen and algae
  • Repair of cracks, breakages and rust
  • Primer to bond and block corrosion
  • Two coats of quality roof paint
  • Choice of colours to suit your home or brand
Materials guide

Typical roof lifespans and care

Indicative figures only. Coastal exposure, workmanship and maintenance all change real-world results.

Roof typeTypical lifespanCare tips
Galvanised IBR / corrugated20-40 yearsCheck fasteners and rusted overlaps, especially near the coast
Colour-coated steel25-40 yearsWash occasionally, touch up scratches early
Concrete tile30-50 yearsRe-bed ridges, repaint every 8-12 years
Clay tile50+ yearsReplace broken tiles, check flashings
Slate75+ yearsRe-fix loose slates and renew lead flashings
Polycarbonate sheeting10-20 yearsChoose UV-protected sheets, avoid abrasive cleaners
Heat-fused membrane (flat roofs)15-25 yearsKeep outlets clear, inspect upstands and seams
Warning signs

When to call a roofer

💧

Stains & damp ceilings

Brown rings, peeling paint or sagging boards.

🔩

Rusty or lifting sheets

Loose screws, rust streaks, lifting at the edges.

🌿

Moss, lichen & granule loss

Retains moisture and accelerates wear.

🌬️

Storm damage

After heavy winter wind and rain.

🪣

Overflowing gutters

Often a blocked outlet or damaged fall.

📈

Rising electricity bills

Poor insulation or ventilation can be the cause.

FAQ

Roofing questions answered

How much does a new roof cost in Cape Town?

Cost depends on size, pitch, material, timber condition and access. We provide a free written quote after inspecting the roof.

Can you repair my roof instead of replacing it?

Often yes. If the structure is sound, repairs and restoration can add many years of life at a fraction of the cost of a new roof.

How long does a roof installation take?

Most houses take several days to two weeks depending on size, weather and structural repairs.

Do you install gutters and clear sheeting?

Yes. We install and repair gutters, downpipes, fascias and UV-protected clear roof sheeting.

Do you work on asbestos roofs?

Older fibre-cement roofs may contain asbestos. Do not drill, break or pressure-wash them. Contact us and we will advise on safe, compliant options.
