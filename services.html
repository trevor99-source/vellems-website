Waterproofing

Waterproofing services: far more than roofs

Waterproofing is the most cost-effective protection a building can have. We apply it wherever water can enter, escape or pool:

  • Roofs and concrete flat decks
  • Timber flat roofs and decks
  • Parapet walls
  • Balconies, terraces and patios
  • Bathrooms and en-suites
  • Showers and wet rooms
  • Kitchens and laundries
  • External and internal walls
  • Basements and lift pits
  • Retaining walls and foundations
  • Swimming pools and water features
  • Water tanks and reservoirs
  • Planter boxes and roof gardens
  • Gutters and box gutters
  • Podium and rooftop decks
  • Damp-proofing and rising damp
