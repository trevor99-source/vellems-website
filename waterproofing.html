Heat-fused membrane

4mm heat-fused membrane: our flagship system

Heat-fused (torch-on) bituminous membrane is fused to a primed substrate to form a continuous, seamless, durable layer. It suits flat roofs, parapets, terraces and podium decks.

  • Strong, flexible and resistant to ponding water and UV (with protective finish)
  • Fully bonded to the deck: no loose sheets lifting in the wind
  • Tested, proven system for domestic, commercial and industrial use
  • Ideal over concrete and prepared timber decks
Systems

Choosing the right waterproofing system

There is no single product for every job. We specify the system to suit the substrate, movement, exposure and finish.

SystemBest forNotes

Parapet walls

Parapet wall waterproofing

Parapet walls are among the most common leak points on flat roofs. Water enters through the top, the inner face, cracks and poorly sealed junctions. We seal the whole detail, not just the visible surface.

  • Repair of cracks, spalling and loose plaster
  • Waterproofing of the upstand where roof meets wall
  • Proper termination and flashing at the top of the membrane
  • Copings sealed or replaced where required
  • Sealing of outlets, scuppers and penetrations
Bathrooms & showers

Bathroom and shower waterproofing

Most bathroom leaks are caused by missing or damaged waterproofing beneath the tiles. By the time stains appear on the ceiling below, damage is already advanced.

  • Floors, shower trays and hobs fully sealed
  • Wall-to-floor junctions and corners reinforced
  • Floor drains and pipe penetrations detailed correctly
  • Wet walls waterproofed to the right height
  • Flood test before tiling so we know it holds
  • Re-waterproofing of leaking showers and bathrooms

FAQ

Waterproofing questions answered

Do you waterproof bathrooms and showers or only roofs?

We do both. We waterproof roofs, flat decks, parapet walls, balconies, bathrooms, showers, basements, retaining walls, pools, tanks and more.

What is a heat-fused membrane?

A 4mm bituminous membrane fused to the deck with heat to form a continuous, durable waterproof layer, ideal for flat roofs, parapets and terraces.

How long does waterproofing last?

Quality systems installed correctly can last many years. We back our work with a three-year guarantee.

Can you waterproof over existing tiles?

Usually the old finish must be removed so the waterproofing can bond to a sound substrate. We assess each case on site.

How much does waterproofing cost?

It depends on area, system, access and preparation. We give free written quotes after inspection.
